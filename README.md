## Why "unblocked"?

Your ex unblocked you just to say this one word — and the word itself is the whole story. No explanation, no apology, no closure: just "unblocked", a tiny status update that somehow says everything about a relationship that ended in silence. It is funny, instantly relatable, and impossible to misread.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only, no network) writes exactly one word — `unblocked`, 9 bytes, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`, after a guard that the word is 1–30 characters and contains no whitespace.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Then check the entry:

```sh
cat /output/entry.txt   # unblocked
```
